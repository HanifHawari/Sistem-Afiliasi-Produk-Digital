## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a focused, high-clarity financial SaaS environment built for Indonesian digital product creators and affiliate marketers. The platform blends institutional authority with agile creator economics. Trust is paramount: affiliates entrust the platform with conversion tracking, split-commission accuracy, and automated payouts via local gateways (BCA, Mandiri, e-wallets, QRIS).

The aesthetic is **Modern Corporate & High-Utility Financial UI**:
- Sharp contrast ratios exceeding WCAG AAA for core financial figures and transaction ledgers.
- Utilitarian density that maximizes screen real estate for data tables, conversion charts, and link management without visual clutter.
- Restrained visual hierarchy where color is strictly semantic: brand authority lives in structured deep indigo, financial progress in growth emerald, and systemic clarity in cool neutral zinc surfaces.

## Colors
The palette balances authoritative deep indigos with vibrant growth greens, anchored on clean slate and zinc surfaces.

### Semantic Tiers
- **Primary Canvas & Action (`#4F46E5` / `#3730A3` / `#1E1B4B`)**: `#4F46E5` governs primary action states, interactive focus bounds, and link affordances. `#1E1B4B` serves as the high-contrast foundational ink for headers, card titles, and high-impact totals.
- **Financial Growth & Commission (`#10B981` / `#059669`)**: Strictly reserved for revenue figures, conversion deltas (`+18.4%`), status indicators (`Berhasil`), and commission yield badges (`Komisi 40%`). `#059669` provides accessible text-contrast thresholds on light tinted surfaces (`#ECFDF5`).
- **Alert & Attention**: Warning states (`Menunggu Verifikasi`) resolve in Amber (`#D97706` over `#FFFBEB`). Rejections (`Ditolak`) and failed disbursements use Crimson (`#DC2626` over `#FEF2F2`).
- **Neutrals (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`, `#E2E8F0`, `#64748B`, `#0F172A`)**: The background foundation relies on crisp pure white (`#FFFFFF`) cards mounted over a light structural wash (`#F8FAFC`). Subdued borders (`#E2E8F0`) provide boundary definition without harsh separation.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans serves as the single font family across display, body, and tabular readouts, offering structural geometric harmony with humanized terminals well-suited for Indonesian corporate and modern tech contexts.

### Currency Formatting Rules (IDR / Rp)
- **Currency Symbol**: The prefix `Rp` is set at the same weight as numeric characters, separated by a non-breaking space (e.g., `Rp 1.450.000`).
- **Tabular Figures**: For tables and live counters, enforce OpenType tabular numerals (`font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1`) to ensure vertical alignment of transaction values across ledger rows.
- **Scale Hierarchy**: Payout balances and total earnings leverage `currency-display` (32px / 800-weight) to provide instant visual anchors inside metric cards.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout implements a rigid 12-column fluid grid system on desktop, collapsing to a 6-column grid on tablet (768px - 1024px), and a single-column layout on mobile (below 768px).

### Grid Behavior
- **Desktop (1280px+)**: Shell constrained to a maximum width of `1440px` with persistent 260px collapsible left-hand navigation. Canvas padding scales to `margin: 2rem` with a `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: Left-hand navigation collapses into an off-canvas drawer or icon rail. Gutter compresses to `1.25rem`. Stat card grids transition from 4-across to 2x2 formations.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: Canvas margin shifts to `1rem` with a `1rem` gap. Complex transaction ledgers horizontal-scroll with a frozen leftmost column (ID/Product) to ensure scannability on handheld screens.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is articulated primarily through **subtle structural outlines and low-opacity ambient shadows**, avoiding heavy skeuomorphism in favor of clear data boundaries.

### Depth Stack
- **Surface Level 0 (Canvas)**: Background tint set to `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation.
- **Surface Level 1 (Data Cards & Panels)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` fill bounded by a crisp 1px outline of `#E2E8F0`. Shadow is an ambient indigo tint: `0 1px 3px rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.02)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Dropdowns & Floating Overlays)**: Surface `#FFFFFF` elevated with `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.04)`. Outlined in `#E2E8F0`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Modals & Drawers)**: Payout modals, bank account linkage dialogs, and QRIS overlays sit above a 40% opacity indigo backdrop (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.5)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`). Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.06)`.

## Shapes
The system applies a disciplined **Level 2 (Rounded)** shape scale. This maintains an approachable contemporary posture while retaining the crisp geometric rigor needed for structured financial tables and dashboards.

- **Standard Inputs, Badges, and Buttons**: `0.5rem` (8px). Matches interaction touchpoints to modern mobile and desktop ergonomics.
- **Cards, Modals, and Stat Panels**: `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px). Creates clean framing for dense data rows.
- **Status Pills and Badges**: Fully rounded (`9999px`) to visually delineate interactive controls (buttons) from non-interactive metadata tags (statuses, commission rates).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Indigo (`#4F46E5`), text white, `roundedness: 0.5rem`. Hover state transitions to `#4338CA`. Active state scales down to 0.98. Focus displays a 2px offset ring of `#818CF8`.
- **Secondary (Destructive / Outline)**: White background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, text `#0F172A`. Hover: `#F8FAFC`.
- **Growth Action**: Solid Emerald (`#059669`), white text, reserved exclusively for high-intent revenue actions (e.g., "Tarik Komisi" / Request Payout).

### Badges & Commission Chips
- **Commission Tag (`Komisi 40%`)**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `0.25rem 0.625rem`. Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#059669`, border 1px solid `#A7F3D0`, font `label-sm`.
- **Status: Berhasil**: Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#059669`, leading 6px circular green indicator dot.
- **Status: Menunggu Verifikasi**: Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#D97706`, leading 6px amber indicator dot.
- **Status: Ditolak**: Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#DC2626`, leading 6px red indicator dot.

### Stat Cards with Delta Indicators
- **Layout**: Surface white, `1rem` padding, rounded-lg (`1rem`), 1px `#E2E8F0` border.
- **Top Row**: Metric label in `body-sm` (`#64748B`) with contextual icon.
- **Center**: Numeric readout in `currency-metric` (`#0F172A`).
- **Footer**: Delta badge featuring an upward arrow + percentage (e.g., `+12.8% vs bulan lalu`), green text (`#059669`) for positive, crimson (`#DC2626`) for negative.

### Referral Link Copier Widget
- **Container**: Flexbox group with unified border. Left side features readonly input displaying the short URL (e.g., `afiliasipro.id/r/kursus-ai?ref=andi`), text color `#475569`, background `#F8FAFC`.
- **Action**: Attached right-aligned button ("Salin Link" / Copy Icon). On click, triggers a quick micro-interaction shifting icon to a green checkmark with a transient tooltip ("Tersalin!").

### Data Tables (Transaction & Commissions Ledger)
- **Header**: `#F8FAFC` background, uppercase `caption` tracking, color `#64748B`, 1px bottom border `#E2E8F0`.
- **Rows**: Alternating hover state `#F8FAFC/60`. Padding `0.875rem 1rem`. Numeric currency columns right-aligned with monospace/tabular numeric rendering.

### Form Controls (Bank Transfer & QRIS Selectors)
- **Input Fields**: 1px border `#CBD5E1`, background white, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`. Padding `0.625rem 0.875rem`. Focus ring `#4F46E5`.
- **Bank Selector Tiles**: Grid cards for Indonesian banks (BCA, Mandiri, BNI, BRI) with official badge logos, 1px border. Selected state triggers a 2px `#4F46E5` border with an upper-right verified badge.
- **QRIS Checkout Container**: Centered canvas card, displaying the dynamic QRIS matrix inside an authenticated frame, real-time countdown timer badge, and automatic confirmation listener status.
